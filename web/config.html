Flight Tracker Setup

Set your WiFi network, home location, and which flights to show on your flight tracker over a USB cable.

This browser can't talk to USB devices

Setup uses the Web Serial feature, which is available in Chrome and Edge on a computer (not Safari, Firefox, or phones). Open this page in one of those browsers.

1. Connect your flight tracker

Plug the flight tracker into this computer with a USB cable, then press Connect. In the list that opens, choose the entry named USB Serial (some boards show CP210x or CH340 instead). It only appears while the board is plugged in.

Don't see it in the list? Show all serial ports.

2. WiFi network

Type your network name, or press Scan to pick from nearby networks (takes about 10 seconds). 2.4 GHz networks only.

Home location

Where the flight tracker itself is - distances and directions on screen are measured from here.
